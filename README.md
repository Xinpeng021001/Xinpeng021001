### About

I am a Ph.D. student in bioinformatics at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, working on **CAZyme annotation** and the **dbCAN** tool ecosystem. My current interests also include **protein language models** and **LLM-assisted** bioinformatics workflows.

- Building and maintaining **run_dbcan** and related microbiome pipelines under [bcb-unl](https://github.com/bcb-unl)
- Exploring structure- and language-model methods beyond classical HMMER / DIAMOND search
- Looking to collaborate on **CAZyme annotation**, **metagenomics**, and **research software**
